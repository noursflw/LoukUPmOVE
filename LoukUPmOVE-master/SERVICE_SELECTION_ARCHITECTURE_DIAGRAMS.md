## Summary

This unified service selection architecture ensures:
- ? **Single Command**: SelectServiceButtonCommand handles all logic
- ? **Dual Collections**: SelectedServices + CurrentBooking.SelectedServices stay synced
- ? **Automatic Updates**: Price and state update automatically
- ? **Consistent UX**: Both pages behave identically
- ? **Clean Code**: Minimal, focused, MVVM-compliant
- ? **Maintainable**: One place to modify logic
